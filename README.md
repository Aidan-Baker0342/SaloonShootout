# Saloon Shootout

Saloon Shootout is a 2D Western-style aim training game created in Unity. The goal is to complete six increasingly difficult rounds as quickly and accurately as possible.

## Gameplay

The player has six bullets per round and must shoot cardboard cowboy targets that appear at different positions and distances.

- 6 rounds of increasing difficulty
- Round 1 starts with 1 target and Round 6 has 6 targets
- 6 bullets per round
- Body hit: 100 points
- Headshot: 200 points
- Randomized target locations
- Targets appear at different sizes to simulate distance
- Running out of ammunition before clearing a round ends the training
- Final results display score, completion time, and round reached
- Retry option after training ends

## Controls

- **Mouse:** Aim
- **Left Click:** Shoot

## Assets

The game uses a combination of custom/AI-assisted artwork and free online sound effects. Sound effects were obtained from Mixkit and include gunshot, empty gun click, and target impact sounds.

## AI Use

ChatGPT was used as a development assistant during the project. It helped with Unity/C# troubleshooting, code implementation and debugging, UI setup, gameplay systems, and development of some visual assets.

AI assistance was used while developing systems including target spawning, rounds, shooting, scoring, ammunition, hit detection, timers, results, and retry functionality. The systems were implemented, tested, adjusted, and integrated into the Unity project during development.
