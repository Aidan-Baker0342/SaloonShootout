SALOON SHOOTOUT - CHANGES FROM DESIGN DOCUMENT

The final game changed somewhat from my original design as I worked on it.

The biggest change was making the game more focused on aim training instead of the original quick-draw/holster idea. The final version has six rounds that get progressively harder. Round 1 has one target, and each round adds another target until Round 6 has six.

Targets spawn randomly at different positions and sizes to simulate different distances. The player gets six bullets each round. Body shots give 100 points and headshots give 200 points.

I also added a score display, ammo display, timer, sound effects, final results screen, and retry button.

AI was used for some help with C# troubleshooting, debugging, and creating visual assets.
