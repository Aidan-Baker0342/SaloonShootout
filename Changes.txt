SALOON SHOOTOUT - CHANGES FROM ORIGINAL DESIGN

The final game stayed close to my original idea but I changed a few things to keep the scope manageable.

The biggest change was removing the holster and DRAW mechanic. Instead, the game focuses directly on the aim-training part of the original idea.

I kept the six-round system, six bullets per round, random target spawning, 100-point body shots, and 200-point headshots. I also made targets appear at different sizes and positions to simulate different distances.

Instead of outlaw characters, I used cardboard cowboy shooting-range targets. I also added an ammo display, score and round UI, a timer, final results screen, and retry button.

For sound, I added a revolver shot, wooden target hit, and empty gun click. I did not add background music.

AI USE

I used ChatGPT for help with some C# troubleshooting and debugging, Unity setup, and creating visual assets for the game.
